The designer–engineer relationship breaks down when engineers hold such low expectations of designers that they don't even expect basic technical awareness from them. The best partnerships happen when both sides bring craft and mutual respect to the collaboration — engineers who expect designers to understand constraints, and designers who earn that expectation.
