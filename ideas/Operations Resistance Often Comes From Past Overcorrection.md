Design teams that resist operations practices — process, measurement, structured workflows — often do so because a previous attempt at operations went too far and became rigidity that killed creative momentum. The memory of bad process can poison the idea of any process. The goal of design operations is not compliance but flow: smooth enough infrastructure that the team can focus energy on the actual design work rather than on coordination overhead.
