An audience cannot meaningfully evaluate a solution if they don't understand the problem it addresses — presenting the solution first forces people to reverse-engineer the context themselves, which produces shallow feedback and misaligned questions. Establishing the problem first primes the audience to evaluate the work on the right terms.
