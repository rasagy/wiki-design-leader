When a design team struggles — chaotic file management, unclear process, mismatched staffing, duplicated work, no measurement — it is often diagnosed as a creative problem ("designers are flaky") when the root cause is operational. Poor operations masquerade as creative dysfunction. Design leaders who ignore operations because they seem unglamorous are leaving one of their most powerful improvement levers untouched.
