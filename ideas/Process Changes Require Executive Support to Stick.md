Expect resistance when introducing new processes — people who don't yet see the value will route around them or quietly revert to old habits. Institutionalizing process changes requires executive sponsorship, not just manager enthusiasm; without visible organizational backing, new processes get treated as optional suggestions rather than durable infrastructure.
