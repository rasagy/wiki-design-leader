Colin Koop (SOM) describes the design leadership role as conductor — taking an orchestra of individual talents and making them work as seamlessly as possible. The implication is that the leader's primary job is not to perform but to arrange: identifying who has the right capability for each design problem and creating the conditions for that talent to be fully expressed.

> "There is so much talent at SOM, and it is our challenge to try to match the talent with the design problem." — Colin Koop, SOM
