Design leaders don't create culture directly — they create the container: the vision, values, and hiring decisions that shape what culture can emerge. Culture is a product of the people you bring in, not the perks you offer.

> "Ultimately, as design leaders, we're building teams and companies, not products and things." — John Torres, America's Test Kitchen
