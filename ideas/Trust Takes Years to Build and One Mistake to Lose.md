Trust in leadership is asymmetric: it accumulates slowly over many consistent actions and can be destroyed by a single bad decision. This asymmetry means leaders must treat every interaction as an investment in a long-running account, and must recognize that the protection offered by accumulated trust is not proportional to the damage a single violation can do.
