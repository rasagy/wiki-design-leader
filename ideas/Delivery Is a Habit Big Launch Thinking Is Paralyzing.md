In a continuous delivery world, perfectionism is a liability — "real artists ship." Delivery is a practice and a habit; the Big Launch mindset, where work is held until it feels complete, is paralyzing and denies the team the real-user feedback that internal iteration can never replicate. Design leaders must recognize the point of diminishing returns and actively push work into the world rather than polishing indefinitely.
