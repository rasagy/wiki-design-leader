Using visible prioritization frameworks (Impact vs. Effort, RICE, etc.) when declining requests makes the decision feel principled rather than arbitrary — stakeholders can see the logic, disagree with the weighting, or adjust the inputs rather than experiencing a personal rejection. Applying these frameworks publicly also builds credibility over time.
