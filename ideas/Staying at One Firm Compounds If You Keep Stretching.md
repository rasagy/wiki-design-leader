Wendy Rogers (LPA) built a 30-year career at a single firm — from intern to CEO — by continuously stretching into new challenges rather than seeking novelty through job changes. Depth of context and long organizational relationships compound in ways that lateral moves cannot replicate; the constraint of staying put forces a different kind of growth than the freedom of leaving.
