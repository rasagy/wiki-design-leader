The seven UX quality factors — Useful, Usable, Findable, Credible, Desirable, Accessible, and Valuable — provide a shared vocabulary for evaluating design decisions and assessing team output. Using this framework in design reviews gives feedback a structured basis beyond personal preference and helps teams understand which quality dimension a critique is targeting.
