Designers fall into two broad archetypes: **Hunters**, who thrive on ambiguity, new products, and open-ended exploration; and **Farmers**, who thrive on constraints, iteration, and refinement. Mismatching a Hunter to a maintenance project or a Farmer to a greenfield one causes frustration and underperformance — knowing which type someone is lets you match them to the right work.
