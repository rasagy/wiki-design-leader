"Management by walking around" — informal, agenda-free check-ins — signals investment in the team without introducing control or surveillance dynamics. When done without an implied audit, these touchpoints build trust and catch small problems early; when done with a hidden evaluative agenda, they create anxiety and teach people to perform only when the manager is watching.
