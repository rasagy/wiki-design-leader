MASS Design Group borrowed the slow food movement's "good, clean, and fair" framework and applied it to architecture: design quality (good), environmental sustainability (clean), and ethical supply chains and labor (fair). The framework is useful beyond its origin context because it translates diffuse ethical commitments into three testable criteria that can be applied to any project decision.
