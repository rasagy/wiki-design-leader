Vision, strategy, and execution are not separate phases that happen in sequence — they are a continuous cycle that the manager is responsible for keeping intact. A clear vision is a prerequisite for good strategy; a good strategy is a prerequisite for effective execution; and the manager's job is to ensure the chain connecting all three is never broken.
