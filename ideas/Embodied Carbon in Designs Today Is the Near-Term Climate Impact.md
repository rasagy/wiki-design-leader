Margaret Montgomery (NBBJ) argues that designers must go beyond minimum code compliance on climate because buildings being designed today won't open until 2028 or later — the embodied carbon in those decisions is locked in now and represents the most significant near-term climate impact, not the operational energy that gets more attention. The design phase is where the carbon budget is spent.
