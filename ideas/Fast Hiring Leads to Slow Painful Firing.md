Rushing a hire to fill a seat feels efficient but creates a delayed, far more expensive problem: managing out someone who was never the right fit. A toxic or mismatched team member poisons the whole group. Slow, deliberate hiring is faster in aggregate than the cost of undoing a bad hire.
