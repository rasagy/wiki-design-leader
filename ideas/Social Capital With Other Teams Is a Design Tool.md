Design quality depends on context that lives outside the design team — in engineering constraints, customer support patterns, sales conversations, and research sessions. Building relationships across functions by showing up in their spaces gives designers richer inputs and converts potential critics into invested collaborators.

> "Your legs are your most effective design tool. Get out and connect with people." — Mark Opland, Facebook
