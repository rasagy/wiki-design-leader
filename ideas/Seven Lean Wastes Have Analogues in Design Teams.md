The seven Lean manufacturing wastes — overproduction, inventory, motion, defects, over-processing, waiting, and transportation — each have direct analogues in how design teams operate. Recognizing these patterns (e.g. waiting on approvals, over-processing through endless revision cycles, unnecessary motion in handoff friction) gives managers a structured lens for diagnosing team inefficiency.
