Hiring for "culture fit" risks encoding existing biases and limiting the team's range of perspectives. Instead, hire for "culture add" — people who bring new viewpoints, backgrounds, and ways of thinking that strengthen rather than replicate what's already there. Define team values collaboratively first so the standard is explicit, not accidental.
