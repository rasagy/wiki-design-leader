Continuous feedback differs from milestone feedback in size, frequency, and specificity — it is tied to specific decisions rather than deliverable reviews, and it arrives frequently enough that course corrections are cheap. Teams that only receive feedback at formal reviews tend to over-invest in directions that need changing, because the feedback arrives too late to be low-cost.
