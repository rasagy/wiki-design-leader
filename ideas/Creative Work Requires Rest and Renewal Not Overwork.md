Creative work cannot be sustained through 60-hour weeks — it requires rest, renewal, and space for growth that overwork systematically destroys. Design leaders who treat team members as interchangeable resources, extracting maximum output without attending to individual needs, are not just managing poorly — they are eroding the very creative capacity that makes the team valuable. People are not widgets; their idiosyncratic strengths and limits must be known and respected.
