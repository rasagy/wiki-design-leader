A single design organization should be responsible for the complete customer journey — from marketing through product through support — spanning interaction design, information architecture, visual design, brand identity, and service design. Fragmenting this across separate teams creates seams in the experience that users feel even if they can't name them. End-to-end experience coherence requires end-to-end design ownership.
