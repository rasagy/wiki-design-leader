When a project is struggling, a manager's instinct is often to focus relentlessly on what's broken — but this accelerates demoralization. A more effective tactic is to identify at least one thing that is genuinely working and explicitly name it for the team, creating a foothold of momentum that makes the problems feel solvable rather than overwhelming.
