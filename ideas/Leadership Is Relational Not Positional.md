The difference between a manager and a leader is not a job title or reporting structure — it is the quality of the relationship between that person and the people they lead. A manager is obeyed because the role requires it; a leader is followed because people want to. Leadership is an emotional and relational achievement that must be earned continuously, not a status conferred once by an org chart.
