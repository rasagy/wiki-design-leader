Servant leadership deploys authority to clear obstacles and enable others rather than to maintain hierarchy or signal status. Its most important effect is not what the leader does but what the team learns to do: when servant leadership is modeled visibly and consistently, teams internalize and replicate it — distributing the leadership function throughout the organization.
