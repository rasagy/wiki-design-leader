Defining success for a major UX redesign is genuinely hard: measurements are muddy, vocal user complaints are unrepresentative of the broader population, and analytics are rarely instrumented correctly. The right approach is to accept the ambiguity, focus on a small number of key metrics, and trust big-picture signals like press coverage, employee morale, and customer satisfaction trends rather than demanding precise quantification.
