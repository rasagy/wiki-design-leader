Customers spend far more time interacting with a product than with any advertisement or marketing campaign, which means product experience — not advertising — is where brand is primarily formed. MailChimp is the exemplar: brand values like fun, casual, and powerful are expressed consistently throughout every interaction, including micro-moments like the chimp's arm that rips off when an email is too wide.
