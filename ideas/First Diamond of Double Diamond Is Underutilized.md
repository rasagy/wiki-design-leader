The Double Diamond framework has two halves: the first diamond (discovery and definition — figuring out the right problem) and the second diamond (development and delivery — building the right solution). Most organizations only engage design in the second diamond, which means design validates strategies that may be fundamentally wrong. Involvement in the first diamond is where design creates its highest-leverage impact.
