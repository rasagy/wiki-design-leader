MailChimp's UX team created a vision video in roughly 10 days before a 4-month redesign; Airbnb worked with Pixar illustrators on storyboards. Powerful design vision artifacts don't require large investments — a focused team can produce something alignment-shifting in days, and the ROI over months of aligned work is enormous.
