Brand is "a person's gut feeling about a product, service, or organization" (Marty Neumeier) — not a logo, color palette, or tagline. This means brand is formed primarily through use, not through advertising. Designers own far more of brand formation than they typically claim, because customers spend orders of magnitude more time with the product than with any marketing material.
