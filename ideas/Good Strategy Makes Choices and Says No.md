The most important attribute of a great strategy is that it makes choices: a strategy that doesn't say no to anything isn't a strategy, it's a wish list. A document that validates every idea and excludes nothing gives no one any basis for deciding how to spend their time.

> "The most important attribute of a great strategy is that it makes choices. A strategy that doesn't say no to anything isn't a strategy."
