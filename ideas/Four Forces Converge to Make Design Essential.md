Four structural forces have made design a business necessity: the ceiling on efficiency optimization, software expanding into every industry, the consumerization of enterprise software (users now expect consumer-grade quality everywhere), and the shift to everything-as-a-service (which demands continuous design, not one-time delivery). Any one of these would justify investment; all four together make it urgent.
