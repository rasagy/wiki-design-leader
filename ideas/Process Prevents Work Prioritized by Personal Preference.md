Without a shared process, work gets sequenced by whoever shouts loudest or by individual designers' personal preferences rather than by project need. Standardized process is a fairness mechanism as much as an efficiency mechanism — it depersonalizes prioritization and makes the criteria for decisions visible to everyone.
