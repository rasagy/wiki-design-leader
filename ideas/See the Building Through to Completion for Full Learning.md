Mariko Masuoka (Pelli Clarke) cautions designers who don't fully appreciate that a building takes five years from start to finish: leaving a project before completion means missing the back half of the design education embedded in that project. Commitment to seeing work through to delivery — not just through design — is where the highest-density learning lives.
