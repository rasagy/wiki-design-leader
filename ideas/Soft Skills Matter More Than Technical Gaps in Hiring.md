When hiring UX designers, empathy, humility, teachability, and openness should outweigh technical gaps — technical skills can be trained, but disposition cannot easily be changed. A designer who lacks a specific tool skill but is genuinely curious and coachable will outperform a technically proficient designer who is defensive or closed to feedback.
