A feedback conversation should feel like a collaboration on how to improve, not a verdict being handed down. When the person receiving feedback feels like a defendant rather than a partner, they focus on defending themselves rather than hearing what's useful — which is the opposite of the outcome the feedback was supposed to produce.

> "The most important thing about giving feedback is to do it. The second most important thing is to do it in a way that actually lands."
