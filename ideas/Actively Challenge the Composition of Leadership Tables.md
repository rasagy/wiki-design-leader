Ana Pinto-Alexander (HKS), upon entering her first leadership meeting, immediately challenged her colleagues: "I guarantee you there are half a dozen remarkable women at HKS — why are they not sitting at the table here with me?" Her approach treats the composition of a room not as a neutral fact but as a deliberate or negligent decision — one that leaders are responsible for correcting on the spot, not over time.
