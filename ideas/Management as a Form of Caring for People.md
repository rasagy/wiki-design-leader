Clay Christensen reframed management not as an administrative role but as one of the most direct ways to help other people — developing their careers, shaping their work lives, and building their capabilities. This reframe shifts management from a bureaucratic burden to a purposeful vocation.

> "I used to think that if you cared for other people you need to study sociology… I concluded if you want to help other people, be a manager." — Clay Christensen, Harvard Business School
