A healthy design culture relies on four distinct feedback formats, each serving a different purpose: **Design Reviews** (detailed critique framed by project goals, max 7 people), **Design Standups** (daily three-question sync, kept short and standing), **Retrospectives** (after every project: what worked / didn't / to improve), and **Postmortems** (after failures: impartial facilitator, focus on events not blame, actions with owners and deadlines).
