Giving and receiving feedback becomes easier over time as psychological safety accumulates within a team. Starting a feedback culture early — even when it's uncomfortable — builds the trust that eventually makes candor feel normal rather than threatening.

> "You know you have a healthy design culture when people are giving each other feedback." — Dustin Senos, formerly Medium
