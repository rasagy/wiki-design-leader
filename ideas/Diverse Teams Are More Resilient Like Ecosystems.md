Diverse design teams are more resilient and productive because diversity functions like biodiversity in an ecosystem — homogeneous systems are fragile; heterogeneous ones can adapt. This reframes DEI from an ethical obligation into a strategic capability: teams that reflect a wide range of backgrounds, perspectives, and disciplines are simply better equipped to solve complex, ambiguous problems.

> "Seek out, hire, and learn from people who are different from you."
