Great UX managers behave like internal headhunters — they are continuously watching for talent in their network, at conferences, and in adjacent teams, so that when a position opens they already have candidates in mind rather than starting from scratch. Reactive hiring under deadline pressure leads to poor decisions; proactive talent-watching builds a pipeline before urgency distorts judgment.
