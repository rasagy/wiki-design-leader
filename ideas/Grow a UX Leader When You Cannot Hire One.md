When a senior UX leader cannot be found externally, the right move is to identify someone internal who lives and breathes customer experience, build a small virtual team around them, and create conditions that let the junior designer earn leadership organically. Manufactured leadership titles imposed from above tend to fail; leadership that grows from demonstrated ownership tends to stick.
