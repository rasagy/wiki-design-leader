Psychological safety in a design team is built not by declaring it, but by senior leaders demonstrating it — by sharing their own work-in-progress and receiving critique with visible humility rather than defensiveness. When leaders only observe critique and never participate as subjects, they create an implicit hierarchy that chills honest feedback for everyone below them. The culture follows the model the leader sets in the room.
