Standups, retrospectives, postmortems, and explicit team values are not bureaucracy — they are scaffolding that frees designers to do their best work by removing ambiguity about expectations and process. Farmers (iteration-oriented designers) in particular need constraints to thrive; structure is a gift, not a cage.
