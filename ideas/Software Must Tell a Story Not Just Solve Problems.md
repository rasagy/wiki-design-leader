The line between software and content is no longer meaningful — software must create emotional connection and tell a story, not merely accomplish a task. Technologists who treat design as decoration miss this shift, relying on foundational design and storytelling techniques that are critical for great user experiences.

> "The line between software and content isn't just blurry, it's irrelevant. But its continued existence makes many technologists eschew the foundational design and storytelling techniques that are critical for making great user experiences."
