Across all sixteen design leaders profiled, an explicit commitment to continuous learning is the one universal trait — from formal internal programs like Gensler University, to mentorship structures, to academic partnerships and research collaborations. Great design leadership is not a destination reached through experience; it is a posture of perpetual inquiry.
