Design leaders should give their team clear priorities, not prescriptive tasks, leaving room for designers to be creative in their own execution. Micromanaging the how undermines ownership and wastes the judgment of experienced people.

> "Hand out priorities, not tasks, and let your people be creative about their own execution." — Kavin Stewart, Reddit
