Sanders identifies "the wild card" as the final leadership value — an acknowledgment that the most consequential qualities of the leaders he interviewed are often the hardest to systematize: idiosyncratic strengths that emerge from a specific biography, background, and risk tolerance that no formula can replicate. Leadership frameworks are useful maps, but the territory always contains something the map cannot capture.
