Quantitative research and usability testing reveal where things break; field research — in-home visits, contextual inquiry, following people through their actual days — reveals why. The two are not substitutes. Field research also builds the kind of deep empathy that makes design solutions actually resonate, rather than just address a logged complaint. Organizations that skip field research are designing against symptoms rather than causes.
