Talent health in a design firm is an ongoing practice captured in four continuous cycles: Recruit, Retain, Reward, and Replenish. Treating talent as a hiring event — something you do when a seat is empty — misses three of the four levers entirely, and produces organizations where good people leave before the organization understands why.
