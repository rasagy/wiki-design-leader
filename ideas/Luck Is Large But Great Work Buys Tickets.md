Most business books ignore luck, but it may account for upwards of 75% of business success — a fact that most success narratives obscure. The correct response is not fatalism but probabilistic thinking: "you don't win the lottery if you don't buy a ticket," so designing great software is what buys tickets. Each excellent product is another entry into the draw.
