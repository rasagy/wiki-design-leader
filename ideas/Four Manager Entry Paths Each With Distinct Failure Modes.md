There are four common paths into a management role, each carrying a distinct failure mode: the Apprentice (promoted from within, risks failing to shift from peer to authority), the Pioneer (building from scratch, risks over-hiring or under-defining the mission), the New Boss (outsider taking over, risks moving before understanding culture), and the Successor (following a predecessor, risks over-correcting from their style). Recognizing which path you're on helps you anticipate the specific trap to avoid.
