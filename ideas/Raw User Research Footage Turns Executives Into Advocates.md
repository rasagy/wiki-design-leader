When executives witness unfiltered user research footage — people struggling with a product, voicing frustration in their own words — they often become design advocates in ways that polished research summaries never achieve. The raw footage creates emotional proximity to the user that a slide deck cannot replicate. Inviting executives to observe or review live research sessions is one of the most effective tools a design leader has for building organizational support.
