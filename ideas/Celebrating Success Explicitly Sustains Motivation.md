A team that only hears feedback when something goes wrong loses motivation for difficult work quickly. Explicitly celebrating success — naming what went well, who contributed, and why it mattered — creates the positive reinforcement that sustains energy through hard projects. This is not about avoiding criticism; it's about ensuring that the ratio of recognition to correction stays healthy.
