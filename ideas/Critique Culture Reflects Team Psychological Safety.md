chapter: "Chapter 10: Continuous Critique"
---

A critique culture that only flows downward — where managers receive only positive feedback — is a symptom of a team that doesn't feel safe enough to critique upward. Healthy critique culture requires that feedback travels in all directions, including from reports to manager.
