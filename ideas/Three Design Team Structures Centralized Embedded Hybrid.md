Design teams can be structured in three ways: **Centralized** (an agency model that's good for craft culture and consistency), **Embedded/EPD** (Engineering-Product-Design cross-functional pods, good for speed and empathy), or **Hybrid** (a context-dependent blend of both). Each structure creates different tradeoffs around coherence, autonomy, and cross-functional alignment.
