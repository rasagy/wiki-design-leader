Optimism is a leadership discipline, not a disposition — and great design leaders distinguish informed optimism (grounded in evidence, research, and honest assessment of constraints) from wishful thinking. The function of optimism is not to deny complexity but to enable action in the face of it; unfounded optimism collapses under pressure, while evidence-based optimism holds.
