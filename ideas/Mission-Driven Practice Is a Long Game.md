Multiple leaders in Sanders's study — Maytum on sustainability and mentorship, Brumfield on equitable urban design, Ricks on the MASS non-profit model — built careers and firms around missions that required decades to bear fruit. The defining characteristic is not patience but sustained commitment: a willingness to be evaluated on outcomes that will only be visible long after the work is done.
