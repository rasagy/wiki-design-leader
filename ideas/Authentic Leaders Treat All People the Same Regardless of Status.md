Authenticity in leadership means your private values and public persona are the same — and a concrete test is whether you treat people who cannot do anything for you the same way you treat clients and executives. Leaders who are warm with power and cold with everyone else reveal a calculated persona rather than a genuine one, and the people closest to them always know it.
