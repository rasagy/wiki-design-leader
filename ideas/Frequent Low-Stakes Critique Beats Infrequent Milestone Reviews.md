chapter: "Chapter 10: Continuous Critique"
---

Regular, low-stakes critique sessions produce better work than high-stakes milestone reviews because frequent small adjustments prevent problems from compounding. When critique only happens at major checkpoints, the cost of changing course rises dramatically — designers become defensive and stakeholders expect polish before feedback is even useful.
