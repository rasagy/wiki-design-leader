The history of graphical interfaces shows a layered accumulation of design expectations: the Mac era (1984) introduced the imperative of consistency; the Web era (1990s) added discoverability; the Mobile era (2000s) added brand and emotional connection. Each new era did not replace the previous imperatives — it added to them.
