Hiring a designer well requires checking five things: formal design training, a strong portfolio where every piece is great (any weak piece reflects poor judgment, not just weak execution), genuine passion for technology, a track record of having shipped something, and above all an amazing attitude — curious, positive, humble, and eager to grow. Attitude is weighted highest because skills can be developed but disposition cannot easily be changed.
