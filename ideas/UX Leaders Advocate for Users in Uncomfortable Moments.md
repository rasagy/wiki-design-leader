UX leadership means advocating for users even when it creates friction with executives, product managers, or engineering — especially then. A UX manager who only raises the user's perspective when it's convenient or uncontested has functionally abdicated the most important part of the role. Leadership credibility is built precisely in the moments when advocacy carries a cost.
