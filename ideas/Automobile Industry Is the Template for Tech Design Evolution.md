The automobile industry offers a precise historical template for where technology companies are headed: Ford resisted design and lost market leadership; General Motors hired Harley Earl and built the Art and Color Section and surpassed Ford as the world's largest manufacturer. Technology companies are making this same transition now, and the outcome will follow the same pattern.
