Reference calls are the most underused tool in hiring — most managers treat them as a formality, but a well-structured reference conversation is often the most honest signal available. Unlike the interview, where the candidate controls the framing, a reference gives you access to someone who has seen the candidate at work without their awareness of being evaluated.
