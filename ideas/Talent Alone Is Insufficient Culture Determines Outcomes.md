The Golden State Warriors won a championship with the same roster that had lost in the first round the prior season — the difference was leadership and culture, not personnel. The same principle applies to design organizations: a team of talented designers without the right culture, shared standards, and empowered leadership will underperform a less individually talented team that has all three.
