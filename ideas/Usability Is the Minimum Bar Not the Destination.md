Calling software "usable" is the equivalent of calling a restaurant's food "edible" — it sets a baseline, not an aspiration. The meaningful space for design work begins above the usability floor, in the territory of emotional experience and brand. Teams that celebrate usability as a success metric have confused the minimum requirement with the goal.
