Culture breaks down when people are set up to fail because work was assigned without considering their actual capabilities. Before distributing work, a UX manager should understand each team member's strengths and weaknesses well enough to match them to tasks where they can succeed — stretch assignments are valuable, but only when the gap is bridgeable with the time and support available.
