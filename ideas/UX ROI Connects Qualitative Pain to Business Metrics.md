Measuring UX return on investment follows a three-step sequence: start with qualitative data to identify users' most common questions and pain points; track the relevant quantitative indicators (traffic, conversions, support tickets, churn); then compare baseline to post-intervention data using consistent tools and timeframes. The qualitative step is often skipped, but without it the quantitative measurements have no narrative to attach to.
