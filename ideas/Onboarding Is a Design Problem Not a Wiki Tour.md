The first 30 days of a new hire's experience set the tone for their expectations, relationships, and understanding of cultural norms — leaving it undesigned means those things form randomly. A structured onboarding plan is not bureaucracy; it is an intentional investment in the employee experience.

> "If you design a bad interview experience, you're already designing a bad employee experience."
