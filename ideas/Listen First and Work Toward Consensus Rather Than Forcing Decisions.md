Sharron van der Meulen (ZGF) leads by listening first and working toward consensus rather than forcing decisions — a style she connects explicitly to her nature as an introvert. This approach is slower but builds ownership across the team; decisions that emerge from genuine consensus are more durable than those imposed from above, because the people who execute them helped shape them.
