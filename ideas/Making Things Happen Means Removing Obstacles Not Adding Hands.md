chapter: "Chapter 8: Making Things Happen"

"Making things happen" as a manager means removing the obstacles in the team's path — not adding your own hands to the execution work. When a manager dives into the work to accelerate delivery, they often slow the team down and signal that they don't trust the people they've hired.
