The modern UX design leader should own the responsibilities that Product Managers currently hold — customer understanding, functionality definition, and product strategy — relegating PMs to project management and outbound marketing. The person with the deepest expertise in creating user experience should be the one defining what that experience needs to be.

> "It must be the goal of every aspiring User Experience Designer to eliminate the need for Product Managers who do anything other than outbound marketing."
