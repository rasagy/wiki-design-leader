When remote team members become guarded, go quiet, or stop raising problems, the correct interpretation is usually insufficient trust rather than disengagement from the work itself. Remote managers who dismiss this signal as "they're just busy" miss an early warning that their communication and trust-building practices aren't reaching everyone.
