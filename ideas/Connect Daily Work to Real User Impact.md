chapter: "Chapter 8: How to Inspire Great Designs and Keep Team Morale High"
---

Designers sustain motivation better when they understand how their daily work affects real people — not just business metrics. Regularly reminding the team of user impact (through user research findings, support feedback, or direct user contact) reconnects the craft to its purpose and provides intrinsic motivation that survives the dry, difficult phases of a long project.
