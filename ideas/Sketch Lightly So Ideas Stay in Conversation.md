Mustafa Chehabbedine (KPF) sketches lightly and disposably so that his ideas remain in conversation rather than becoming fixed commitments that foreclose other possibilities. When a leader's early concepts look too finished, they stop being proposals and become positions — and the team shifts from co-creating to reacting.
