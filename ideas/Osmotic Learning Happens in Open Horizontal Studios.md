Marsha Maytum (Leddy Maytum Stacy) designed her studio as open and horizontal specifically to enable osmotic learning — the kind of knowledge absorption that happens through proximity and observation rather than formal instruction. Culture designed for ambient learning compounds over time; hierarchical or siloed structures require explicit knowledge transfer that is always slower and less complete.
