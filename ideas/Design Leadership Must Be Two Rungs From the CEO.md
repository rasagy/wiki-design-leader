Design leadership must sit one or two organizational rungs from the CEO to be effective. Distance from the C-suite makes it easy for other functions to dismiss design's contribution as tactical, and makes end-to-end experience work politically impossible — because experience crosses organizational boundaries that only senior executives can arbitrate. Org chart position is a structural prerequisite for design's influence, not just a status symbol.
