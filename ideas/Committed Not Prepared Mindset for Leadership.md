Waiting to feel fully prepared before stepping into leadership is a trap — no one is ever 100% ready. The right posture is total commitment despite incomplete readiness, combined with proactive research into what the role actually demands.

> "I'm never going to be 100% prepared to be a design leader, but I'll always be 100% committed." — Andy Law, Netflix
