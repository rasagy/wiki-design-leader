The right level of oversight for any designer depends on their experience level, the project's visibility and stakes, deadline pressure, and team stability — not a universal rule applied equally to everyone. Applying the same level of scrutiny to a senior designer as to someone three months into their first role signals a failure to see people as individuals, and often drives away the most capable people.
