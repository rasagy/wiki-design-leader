When a designer becomes a manager, their identity must shift from maker to multiplier — success is no longer measured by the quality of their own designs, but by the quality of the designs their team produces. The skills that made someone a great designer have limited transfer value to management, and clinging to maker identity actively hinders the team.

> "You will not be evaluated on the quality of your own designs. You will be evaluated on the quality of the designs your team produces."
