T-shaped professionals have deep expertise in one discipline — the vertical bar — paired with broad enough fluency across adjacent fields to collaborate effectively — the horizontal bar. Hiring intentionally for this shape builds teams where depth creates quality and breadth creates the connective tissue between disciplines.
