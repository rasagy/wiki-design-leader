Effective design organizations have a single, clear line of design authority — not a committee of peers with diffuse accountability. At scale, this can split into a Creative Director (responsible for vision and quality) and a VP of Design (responsible for operations and hiring), mirroring the CTO/VP Engineering split in engineering. Clarity of authority is what makes accountability possible and decision-making fast.
