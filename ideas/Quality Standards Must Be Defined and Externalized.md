Design quality cannot be left to subjective taste or the judgment of whoever is in the room — it must be defined, documented, and externalized so that the whole organization can reference the same standard. Without explicit quality criteria, every critique becomes a negotiation, and every shipping decision becomes a fight. A shared, visible quality bar also gives design leaders the standing to refuse work that doesn't meet it.

> "A design organization is only as good as what it delivers, and if it is producing crap because it's trying to do too many things, then the rest of the organization will associate design with crap."
