The most effective argument for design authority is never a presentation or a process proposal — it is the work itself. Making something so demonstrably good that resistance becomes untenable is how designers destroy opposition and raise the bar for the entire industry. Every excellent product creates new incentives for competitors and colleagues alike to follow suit.

> "Every product you put out, every experience you create, that is better designed, more customer-centric, and even more thoughtful, will raise the bar further, and create incentives for the rest of the industry to follow suit."
