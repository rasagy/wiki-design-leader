Across 100 interviews, negotiation, conflict resolution, and presentation skills were consistently cited as both high-leverage and chronically underdeveloped in design leaders. Design training cultivates craft but rarely addresses the interpersonal skills that leadership actually demands — these must be sought out deliberately.
