The manager's job, stripped to its core, is to get better outcomes by influencing three things: purpose (does the team know what they're trying to achieve?), people (do they have the skills and motivation?), and process (do they have a way of working that lets them succeed?). Everything else a manager does is in service of one of these three levers.
