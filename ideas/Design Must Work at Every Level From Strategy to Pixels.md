A design organization must be capable of operating at every level of scale: from 10,000 feet (integrated company strategy, product vision) down to 1 foot (typography, spacing, transitions). No single designer spans this full range, which means even early-stage design hiring should bring in people with complementary altitude. Design that operates only at the surface level will never earn a seat at the strategy table.
