The ideal UX designer is a "singer-songwriter" — someone who both composes (interaction design, product definition) and performs (visual design, execution). Splitting these responsibilities into separate roles — one designer for thinking, another for making — typically produces worse outcomes because the feedback loop between conceiving and crafting is where the best decisions happen.
