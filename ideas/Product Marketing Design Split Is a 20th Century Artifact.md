The historical separation of "product design" and "marketing design" into distinct teams is a 20th-century manufacturing artifact — it made sense when products were physical and marketing was broadcast, but it doesn't hold in a services world where the product and the communication about the product are the same continuous experience. Organizations that maintain this split are optimizing for a model that no longer fits the medium.
