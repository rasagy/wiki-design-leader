For remote teams, weekly video calls are the minimum viable investment in face-to-face connection — text-only async communication erodes trust over time and generates ambiguity that compounds across time zones. The visual and tonal cues of video are not a luxury; they are the partial substitute for the ambient co-location signals that in-person teams receive for free.
