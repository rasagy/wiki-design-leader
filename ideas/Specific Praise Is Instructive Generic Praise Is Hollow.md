Positive feedback matters as much as corrective feedback, but only when it's specific. Generic praise ("great job") is warm but not useful; specific praise ("the way you framed the tradeoffs in that meeting made the decision much easier for everyone") tells the person exactly what behavior to repeat and why it worked.
