Designers who speak only the language of craft — aesthetics, usability, interaction — are limiting their organizational standing. When designers can articulate their work in terms of conversion rates, engagement, churn, and brand equity, and when they proactively propose ways to unlock new business value (not just execute against a given brief), the design function earns credibility as a strategic peer rather than a delivery service.
