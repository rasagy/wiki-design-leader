Leadership and management are distinct capabilities: leaders inspire people toward common goals, while managers plan and monitor execution against those goals. The best UX managers do both — they set a vision that motivates and they run the operational machinery that makes progress tangible. Knowing which mode the situation calls for is itself a management skill.
