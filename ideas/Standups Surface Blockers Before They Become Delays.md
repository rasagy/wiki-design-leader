Daily standups of 5–10 minutes maximum help surface blockers while they are still small — a problem mentioned in standup today can be resolved before it causes a day of lost work tomorrow. The time limit is essential: enforcing it with a timer keeps standups from becoming status theater and preserves their value as a fast-moving signal check.
