Product roadmaps tell teams what to build and when, but only a design vision tells them why. Without a compelling "why," teams optimize for milestones rather than meaning — and lose their mission as scope grows and sprints accelerate.

> "People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it." — Simon Sinek (cited by Walter & Woolery)
