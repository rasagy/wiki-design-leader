In engineering-led companies, designers and marketers are natural allies who rarely realize it. Designers can build this relationship strategically by doing design favors for the marketing team — small, high-impact work that earns goodwill. Once marketers see designers as allies rather than gatekeepers, they become advocates for design influence inside the broader organization.
