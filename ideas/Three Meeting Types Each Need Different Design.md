There are three core meeting types — decision-making, brainstorming, and information-sharing — and each has different success criteria and design requirements. Running a decision-making meeting like a brainstorm, or an information-sharing meeting like a decision meeting, produces the frustration of misaligned expectations before anyone has said anything wrong.
