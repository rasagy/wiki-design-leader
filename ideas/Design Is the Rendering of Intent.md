Jared Spool's definition — "design is the rendering of intent" — is deliberately broad: it includes not just craft execution but also strategy, vision, and organizational direction. A design leader who accepts this definition can legitimately claim a seat at the strategy table; one who defines design as "making things look good" cannot.
