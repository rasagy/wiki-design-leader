source: "[[The Making of a Manager - What to Do When Everyone Looks to - Zhuo, Julie (2019)]]"
chapter: "Chapter 1: What Is Management?"
---

Management is not about control or doing the work yourself — it's about creating the conditions for the team to do its best work. The shift from individual contributor to manager is a shift from making things to enabling others to make things.

> "The job of a manager is not to do the work yourself. It's to create the conditions for others to do great work."
