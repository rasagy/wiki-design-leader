Multiple design leaders in Sanders's study describe the shift from DEI awareness to DEI action as a structural one — actually restructuring hiring pipelines, promotion criteria, and meeting dynamics to bring underrepresented voices to the table, rather than expressing values and waiting for the culture to change. Awareness without structural change is virtue signaling; structural change without awareness is fragile.
