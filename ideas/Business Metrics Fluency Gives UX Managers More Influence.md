---

UX managers who can connect design decisions to business outcomes — traffic, conversion, churn, support volume — have significantly more organizational influence than those who speak only in design terms. Industry benchmarks (Walmart saw 200%+ traffic increase after a UX redesign; HubSpot saw 300% conversion improvement) are useful for building internal credibility before you have your own data.
