Values are not what's written on the wall — they are what gets rewarded when no one is watching. The gap between stated values and rewarded behavior is the gap between the culture a team claims to have and the culture it actually has.

> "The culture of a team is the behaviors that the team repeatedly exhibits. If you want a different culture, you need different behaviors."
