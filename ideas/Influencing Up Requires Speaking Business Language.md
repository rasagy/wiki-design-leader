To influence people above you in an organization, you must speak their language — business outcomes, risk, timeline, and competitive positioning — rather than design craft vocabulary. Presenting design thinking in design terms to executives is the single most common reason good design arguments fail to land.

> "Your job is not to be right. Your job is to make the right thing happen."
