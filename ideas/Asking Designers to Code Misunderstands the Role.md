Silicon Valley's desire for "designers who can code" reflects a fundamental misunderstanding of what designers contribute. A great designer's time spent fighting CSS browser inconsistencies is a waste of design capacity, not a sign of versatility. Technical awareness is valuable; technical implementation is not where designer leverage lives.
