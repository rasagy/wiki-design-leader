When clients say a firm's fees are too high, the real diagnosis is almost always that the firm failed to differentiate its value clearly enough — not that the fees are objectively excessive. Competing on price is a race to the bottom; competing on differentiation creates a client experience that makes price secondary to the relationship and outcomes.
