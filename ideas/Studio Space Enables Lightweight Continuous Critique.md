Physical studio space — whiteboards, wall space for posted work, dedicated project rooms — supports the kind of continuous, low-stakes design critique that makes work better over time. When work is visible on walls, impromptu check-ins happen naturally; when it's hidden in files, critique only happens at scheduled milestones. The environment is infrastructure for the culture: designing a space for continuous feedback is itself an act of organizational design.
