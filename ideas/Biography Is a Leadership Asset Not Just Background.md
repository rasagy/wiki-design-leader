Across every leader profiled, personal biography — immigration, introversion, coming up through a single firm, a background in research, or experience of marginalization — directly shapes their leadership strengths in ways that are inseparable from who they are. Leadership development that ignores biography and focuses only on skills misses the most durable source of differentiated leadership capability.
