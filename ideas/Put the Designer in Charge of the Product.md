The goal of a technology company is not to write code or make money — it is to create a user experience that is indispensable, delightful, and special. It follows that the person in charge should be the person with the deepest expertise in creating that experience. Putting designers in leadership positions is not a political preference; it is an organizational logic that follows from what technology companies are actually for.
