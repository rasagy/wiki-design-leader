Being too harsh and being too lenient are mirror failure modes in management — harshness creates fear that suppresses honest communication, while leniency signals that standards don't matter and removes the accountability that enables self-organization. Calibrated feedback that holds a real bar without punishing is harder to deliver but the only approach that produces a healthy culture.
