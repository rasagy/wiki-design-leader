Modern product delivery requires three equal legs of authority: business, technology, and design. A team strong in two but weak in one wobbles — technically sound products can fail without user insight, and well-designed ideas without technical feasibility don't ship. Design leadership must claim its seat at this triad as an equal partner, not a downstream consultant.
