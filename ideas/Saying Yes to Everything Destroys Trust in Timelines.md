chapter: "Chapter 12: Saying No"
---

When a team consistently accepts every request, their commitments stop meaning anything — stakeholders learn that estimates are negotiable fictions rather than real forecasts, which erodes trust in the team's planning over time. Saying no selectively is what makes yes credible.
