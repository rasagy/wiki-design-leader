Billie Faircloth (KieranTimberlake) frames her research leadership through innovation metrics rather than productivity metrics, arguing that in genuinely exploratory work, the quality of the process is more meaningful than its measurable outputs. When innovation is measured like production, teams optimize for visibility over discovery.

> "You trust the workflow and the process more than the metrics themselves." — Billie Faircloth
