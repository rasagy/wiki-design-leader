Several of the most impactful design leaders in Sanders's study — including Billie Faircloth at KieranTimberlake, Marsha Maytum at Leddy Maytum Stacy, and Alan Ricks at MASS — lead small, deeply specialized teams that produce outsized impact relative to their size. Focus and depth of shared purpose create a kind of force multiplication that large, broad teams rarely achieve, even with more resources.
