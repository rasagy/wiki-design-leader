The manager who doesn't know how they respond under pressure will be surprised by themselves at the worst possible moments. Understanding your own triggers and behavioral patterns — through coaching, journaling, or peer feedback — is not self-indulgence; it's a prerequisite for not transmitting your dysfunctions to the team.
