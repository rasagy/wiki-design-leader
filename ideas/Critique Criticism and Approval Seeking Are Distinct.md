Critique (structured feedback toward a goal), criticism (negative judgment of the work or person), and approval-seeking (presenting work to get validation rather than input) are three distinct modes that most teams conflate. Naming which mode a conversation is operating in is the first step to making it productive.

> "Critique is a gift. It takes time, attention, and care. Treat it accordingly."
