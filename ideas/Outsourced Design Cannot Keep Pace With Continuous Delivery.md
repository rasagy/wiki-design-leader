Outsourced or agency design cannot match the cadence of continuous delivery, nor can it internalize the company's evolving values, context, and constraints. Bringing design in-house is no longer optional for companies that ship continuously — the cost of context transfer and the latency of external engagement compound into a competitive disadvantage over time.
