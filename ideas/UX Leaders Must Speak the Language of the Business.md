Turning yourself into a UX leader requires expanding beyond design fluency: you must understand business metrics as well as design metrics, and be able to speak the language of engineers, marketers, and executives. Design leaders who stay inside design vocabulary cede influence; those who take on responsibilities beyond the brief earn authority.
