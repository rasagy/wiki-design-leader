Low-fidelity sketches and concept models surface incoherent strategies at the point when changing course is still cheap. If design enters only during development, incoherence gets discovered when engineering is already underway — making it expensive to correct. The value of early, rough design work is less about aesthetics and more about stress-testing strategy before anyone has invested heavily in it.
