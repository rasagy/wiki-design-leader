Trust on a design team is built through two concrete behaviors: sharing information freely so that team members understand the context behind decisions, and giving people real decision-making authority within their scope. Teams where information is hoarded and all decisions flow through the manager develop learned helplessness and lose the ability to self-organize under pressure.
