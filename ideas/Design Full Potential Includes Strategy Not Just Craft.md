Defining design as "problem-solving" is reductionist — it captures only the execution end of design's range. Design's full potential includes problem-framing (defining what problem is worth solving), strategy (shaping what a company pursues), and injecting humanism into organizations. Leaders who accept the narrow definition limit design's organizational role before it has a chance to expand.
