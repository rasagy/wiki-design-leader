question: "What are the key responsibilities of a design leader?"
---

## Answer

A design leader's core job is to create the conditions in which a team can do its best work — not to be the best designer in the room. This question touches on **4 areas**:

### Shifting from Maker to Leader

The transition into leadership strips away the craft work that earned you the role and demands an entirely different skillset. Individual contributor abilities don't transfer — they must be deliberately replaced.

- [[ideas/Leadership Transition Requires an Entirely New Skillset]]
- [[ideas/Design Leader Conducts the Orchestra Not Plays]]
- [[ideas/Design Leaders Can Stay Close to Craft and Strategy]]
- [[ideas/Soft Skills Are Perennially Underdeveloped in Design Leaders]]

### Building and Managing the Team

The team becomes your primary output. Hiring, developing individuals, setting culture, and delegating priorities (not tasks) are the daily mechanisms of leadership.

- [[ideas/Five Core Responsibilities of Design Leadership]]
- [[ideas/Your Team Is Your Primary Customer as a Leader]]
- [[ideas/Hand Out Priorities Not Tasks]]
- [[ideas/Lead by Example but Delegate Intentionally]]
- [[ideas/Delegation Requires Building Trust First]]
- [[ideas/Leaders Create the Container Culture Fills It]]

### Forging Organizational Alliances

Design quality depends on context that lives outside the design team. Building relationships across functions and making design work visible turns stakeholders into advocates.

- [[ideas/Educating the Company About Design Builds Allies]]
- [[ideas/Social Capital With Other Teams Is a Design Tool]]
- [[ideas/Visibility Is a Strategic Act for Design Leaders]]

### Shaping Vision and Strategy

Without a compelling "why," teams optimize for milestones rather than meaning. Design leaders are responsible for articulating the vision that gives direction its purpose.

- [[ideas/Roadmaps Show What Vision Shows Why]]
- [[ideas/Story Is the Most Effective Medium for Design Vision]]

> [!note] From notes
> The core responsibilities, as distilled from interviews in *Design Leadership Handbook*, are: **building the team, managing individuals, operationalizing process, forging alliances, and shaping vision** — and the authors stress that neglecting any one of them weakens the whole. [[notes/Design Leadership Handbook - Aarron Walter and Eli Woolery (2019)]]
